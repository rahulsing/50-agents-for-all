# CloudNote Help Center (sample data)

## Plans and pricing
CloudNote Free includes 3 notebooks and 100 MB storage. CloudNote Plus costs 6 USD per month and includes unlimited notebooks, 10 GB storage and offline mode. CloudNote Team costs 12 USD per user per month and adds shared workspaces and admin controls.

## Billing and refunds
You are billed at the start of each month. You can cancel any time from Settings > Billing; access continues until the end of the paid month. Refunds are available within 14 days of a charge if you have not used Plus features in that period. Refunds take 5-7 business days.

## Password reset
Go to the sign-in page and click "Forgot password". The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If no email arrives, check spam, then contact support. Accounts with two-factor authentication need a backup code to reset.

## Sync problems
If notes do not sync, check that you are on the latest app version, then sign out and sign in again. Offline edits sync automatically when you reconnect. Conflicting edits create a copy named "conflicted copy" so nothing is lost.

## Exporting data
You can export any notebook as Markdown or PDF from the notebook menu > Export. Team admins can export the whole workspace as a ZIP file. Exports of more than 1 GB are emailed as a download link.

## Supported platforms
CloudNote runs on Windows 10+, macOS 13+, iOS 16+, Android 11+ and in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Linux is supported through the web app only.
